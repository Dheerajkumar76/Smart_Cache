How it decides what to keep

When the cache is full, every stored answer gets a score, and the lowest one makes room.

keep score = AI cost × predicted reuse

  • Predicted reuse comes from a small neural network trained with reinforcement learning on how soon each answer was needed again.
  • It scores answers, not slots, so one trained model works for a cache of 30, 100 or 300 answers.
  • A safety guard watches a shadow LRU cache and switches to it if the learned policy ever does worse.
